# Kintovo mark and usage

The supplied pitch PDF contains a small coral symbol before the opening headline, but no separate vector logo file or construction specification. The mark in `public/brand/` is an interpretation from the visible slide reference: three short left strokes converge into a rising diagonal, with a final coral dot. It uses the design-system accent `#E45A3D` and is paired with a restrained uppercase wordmark.

## Assets

- `kintovo-mark.svg` — transparent symbol only.
- `kintovo-lockup.svg` — coral symbol with ink wordmark for light surfaces.
- `kintovo-lockup-dark.svg` — coral symbol with white wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `app/icon.svg` — application icon on paper background.

## Minimum use and spacing

- Use the horizontal lockup at 120 px wide or larger.
- Use the symbol alone at 24 px or larger; the application icon is a separate 48 px viewBox.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the dot diameter in the chosen size.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, or place the coral mark over a low-contrast background.
- Use the light lockup on paper or white surfaces and the dark lockup on ink/dark surfaces.

These sizing rules are conservative working assumptions pending review by the brand owner against the source artwork.
